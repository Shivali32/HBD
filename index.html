Hey Buddy,

Happy Birthday! ❤️

Honestly, I don’t know how we completed these three years. When I actually sit and think about our journey, it feels like a complete roller coaster, bro.

The first year was basically the journey from friends → best friends → boyfriend-girlfriend. And honestly, shifting from being best friends to being in a relationship wasn’t easy. We knew each other so well as friends, but suddenly being a couple was a completely different thing. There were so many things to understand, adjust to and learn.

Then came the second year. We were actually a proper couple by then. 😂 Half of that year was long-distance relationship, and the other half was spent thinking, “Okay, now he’ll finally come back from the UK and this long-distance thing will end.”

But life had other plans.

UK ended, but Mumbai-Pune started. 😂

Still, somewhere in that year, our understanding became stronger. We started feeling more like an actual couple, understanding each other’s expectations, habits, moods and everything that comes with it.

And then came this third year.

Fully roller coaster.

There were small arguments, hurtful moments, situations that were difficult to cope with and memories that I honestly wish we could handle differently. From my side, I know I was too emotional at times. Managing personal things along with corporate life, work pressure and everything else wasn’t always easy. There were differences in our opinions too.

There were quite a few moments this year when things were genuinely difficult.

But there is one thing I’m really proud of.

We never broke our rule.

The rule was simple — we wouldn’t go to sleep angry. Whatever the fight, whatever the issue, whatever happened, we had to talk about it and solve it before sleeping.

And I’m genuinely happy that we followed it.

Every single time, somehow, we talked. We understood. We sorted things out. Maybe not immediately, maybe not perfectly, but we never just left things hanging.

And I think that matters a lot.

This year also changed a lot of things for us.

We told our parents on an election day. 😂 Basically, we took a bahumat on our relationship.

And after that, we finally told our friends too — especially the people I had been wanting to tell for the last three years. And honestly, I was so happy seeing how sportingly everyone took it.

And now, on 18th September — or technically 17th September for some people — even some of our extended family knows about us.

So yeah…

Families are involved now, bro. 😂

I think now we really need to start focusing on ourselves too. Not just professionally, but as grown-up individuals. We have reached that stage where we need to think about our future, our finances, our health, our careers, our families and everything else that comes with growing up.

I have already started thinking about a few things.

Apart from the usual corporate-professional life, I want to explore some other source of income too. I don’t exactly know how or where to start yet, so I’ll definitely need your suggestions and help there.

And one more thing —

I want to lose weight by December.

Don’t know how exactly I’m going to do it. 😂 But I need your motivation, bro.

And actually, I would love it if we both started something like this on the same day. We can decide a target, give ourselves a specific period and then check the results together.

It would be really nice if, at the end of it, we could say, “Okay, we actually did this together.”

Because I think this is something I want from us now — not just memories and trips and birthdays, but also doing things together that genuinely make us better.

One thing I realised about myself this year is that my patience level has gone down tremendously. 😂 I get angry at the smallest things. I’ve been fighting with a lot of things on a personal level too.

And through all of this, you have always been there.

Sometimes I genuinely get confused about my own decisions. I keep thinking, “Was that the right decision? Should I have done something differently?”

But thank you for understanding me.

Thank you for not giving up on me.

Honestly, I haven’t seen such an understanding person who can handle this much nonsense from his girlfriend. 😂

And now comes something I really wanted to tell you.

This year, I think I missed my old best friend the most and not just my boyfriend.

I really needed Hoodie this year by my side. I know it’s probably very difficult to go backwards from being my boyfriend to being my “best friend Hoodie,” and honestly, it’s probably not even fair of me to ask for that.

But bro, I really needed that person.

Someone with whom I could just talk. Someone I could tell random things to. Someone who could listen to my friend problems, my nonsense, my confusion and everything without everything necessarily becoming a “relationship conversation.”

This year, I did find two new friends. But as usual, my brain is constantly confused. 😂 Because of everything that has happened in the past, there is always this fear in my head — “What if something goes wrong again?”

I overthink friendships. I overthink people. I overthink situations.

And thank you for understanding that.

Thank you for understanding that I need friends in my life.

Especially when I’m alone in Mumbai.

Thank you for never making me choose between my relationship and my personal life.

You have no idea how much that matters to me.

Travelling is important to me. My friends are important to me. My personal space is important to me.

And I know you want to travel too, so we definitely need to plan something for ourselves. ❤️

But at the same time, thank you for never stopping me from going somewhere alone with my friends.

Thank you for trusting me enough to let me go wherever I want, with whoever I want, and enjoy my own life.

I genuinely appreciate that.

In a world where relationships can sometimes become about restrictions, permissions and constantly questioning each other, the trust you have shown me means a lot.

And I promise you one thing — I will always tell you.

Where I’m going, who I’m going with, what I’m doing — that’s my responsibility. Freedom doesn’t mean hiding things from each other.

I want you to know everything because I want that trust to stay exactly the way it is.

So thank you, bro.

For trusting me.

For understanding me.

For giving me freedom.

For letting me have my own life while still being a part of yours.

And now, I genuinely can’t wait to see what this next year brings for us.

More surprises, more travelling, more stupid fights, more laughter, more plans, more memories — and hopefully a little less unnecessary drama. 😂

But this time, I want us to actually make some plans and fulfil them.

Maybe we can set a few targets for ourselves for the coming year. Personal targets, financial targets, health targets, travel plans — anything.

And before our next birthday, we should be able to look back and say,

“Okay, bro. We actually did it.”

And next year, I really hope we can plan something for our families too. Something that makes them happy and proud of us. Something that makes them feel that whatever journey we have chosen, we are actually growing together and building something meaningful.

Three years ago, we were just two best friends who somehow ended up here.

Today, we have our families involved, our friends know, we have survived long distance, Mumbai-Pune, arguments, misunderstandings, corporate stress, personal chaos and a lot of nonsense. 😂

And somehow…

We are still here.

So…

Happy Three to Us, Punnu. ❤️

And Happy Birthday once again, Buddy.

Here’s to another year of figuring out life together, one stupid decision, one fight, one trip and one memory at a time.

Love you, bro. ❤️
