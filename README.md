# 🧱 Happy Birthday Pranav! • Lego-Themed Adventure Website

An interactive, 5-chapter Lego-themed birthday web experience celebrating Pranav's special day and chronicling our relationship journey.

## 🌟 Features & Chapters

1. **Homepage (The Hook)**
   - Sky-blue Lego stud baseplate theme.
   - Lego brick banner: `"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, PRANAV!"`.
   - Custom Lego minifigure couple scene connecting **Pune ✈️ London ✈️ Mumbai**.
   - Glowing 3D Lego brick CTA button: `"BEGIN OUR ADVENTURE!"`.

2. **Our Story (Interactive Timeline)**
   - Industrial grey baseplate theme.
   - Milestone cards:
     - **5–6 Years Ago:** Pune CAT Classes & Desk Dates
     - **4 Years Ago:** London MBA & Long Distance Calls
     - **3 Years Ago → Today:** Mumbai Work & Reunited
   - Interactive decision blocks with memory reveals.

3. **Travels (Interactive Lego World Map)**
   - Classic green Lego baseplate theme.
   - Interactive landmark pins for **Paris**, **Amsterdam**, **Rome**, **London**, and **Pune & Mumbai**.
   - Realistic 3D tilted Polaroid photo frames with travel dates and romantic captions.

4. **The Relationship Quiz (The Playful Loop)**
   - Pastel pink baseplate with Lego whiteboard framing.
   - Question: *"DIDN'T THOSE LONG DISTANCE CALLS MAKE US STRONGER?"*
   - **The Runaway "NO" Button:** Dodges across the screen with cheeky banter dialog, while the green **"YES!"** button grows bigger until clicked!

5. **The Birthday Surprise (Grand Finale Reveal)**
   - Celebratory golden baseplate with exploding Lego present box.
   - **"REDEEM MY SURPRISE TRIP!"** rainbow brick button.
   - Full-screen custom Lego brick & stud canvas confetti cannon.
   - Unfolds a luxury **Golden Boarding Pass** with trip details and a personal birthday love letter.

---

## 🎵 Audio Engine
Includes an offline synthesizer powered by the **Web Audio API** featuring snappy Lego brick click sounds, playful runaway whistles, success chimes, and a birthday fanfare melody.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally
Double-click `index.html` in any web browser. No dependencies, npm install, or build tools required.

## 🌐 Deploy to GitHub Pages
To view this live online:
1. Go to your repository settings on GitHub: **Settings > Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment > Source**, select `Deploy from a branch`.
3. Choose `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
